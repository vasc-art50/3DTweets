  - title: Anti-backlash POM vs. brass nuts
    text: Standard brass trapezoidal nuts have backlash. When the lead screw flexes, that mechanical play shows up as visible Z-wobble on tall prints. We compared that with POM anti-backlash nuts, which apply preload and reduce free motion without needing frequent lubrication.
    column: is-6
  - title: Digital GT2 belt tension meter
    text: Uneven belt tension in a CoreXY machine can skew the geometry and create circularity errors. We built a simple tension meter using a strain gauge from a kitchen scale, 3D-printed parts, and a spring system to keep both belts balanced and repeatable.
    column: is-6
