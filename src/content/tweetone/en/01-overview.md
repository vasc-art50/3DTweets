TweetONE began as a family engineering project built around a simple challenge: take the most useful parts of two older Creality Ender 3 machines and turn them into a fast, stiff, and precise CoreXY platform designed for real workshop use.

The concept drew inspiration from the Duender CoreXY, Ender NG, Mercury One.1, and RatRig systems, but pushed further with linear rails, triple-Z motion, a CANbus toolboard, and a modern Klipper toolchain. The target was not just a clean printer, but a machine that balances print quality, rigidity, and usable speed.
